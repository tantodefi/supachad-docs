# Get started

Chad is an always-on AI agent. You can **use the hosted app** in under a minute,
or **self-host** the whole stack. Most people start hosted.

## Use the hosted app

1. Go to **[supachad.com](https://supachad.com)** and pick a plan (Free needs no card).
2. Enter your email to create your account. We provision your login and send an
   access link.
3. Open **[chad.supachad.com](https://chad.supachad.com)** and start chatting.

[:octicons-arrow-right-24: Launch the app](https://chad.supachad.com){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[:octicons-arrow-right-24: Sign up](https://supachad.com#get-started){ .md-button }

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0 | 50 messages a day on Chad Lite, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Web search + per-user memory. No card. |
| **Credits** | Pay-as-you-go | Buy packs of 1,000 or 5,000 credits. 1 credit = 1 message, no daily cap, credits don't expire. Secure checkout via Square. |
| **Premium** | Subscription | The full **Chad** agent — unlimited messages, long-term memory, automations, and sub-agents. |

## How the free tier and credits work

- Every account gets a **daily free allowance** (50 messages) that resets at
  midnight UTC. The app shows how many you have left.
- When the free allowance runs out, you're prompted to **buy credits** or
  **upgrade to premium** — you don't have to wait for the reset.
- **Credits** are pay-as-you-go: while you hold a balance, each message spends one
  credit and the daily cap doesn't apply. Credits never expire.
- **Premium** removes limits entirely and unlocks the full agent (memory,
  automations, sub-agents), not just the lightweight chat model.

Payments are handled by **Square** hosted checkout — Chad never sees your card.

The entire stack is open and reproducible — the sandbox blueprint, the network
